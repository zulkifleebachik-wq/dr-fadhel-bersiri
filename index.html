Siri Kemukjizatan al-Qur'anThe Miracle of the Qur'an Series

OlehBy Prof. Dr Fadil Salih as-Samarra'ial-Samarra'i

Klip ringkas daripada pakar bahasa al-Qur'an, bersama nota renungan dwibahasa untuk direnung dan diamalkan.

Short clips from a master of Qur'anic language, with bilingual reflection notes to ponder and act on.

Tentang penceramah: lahir 1933 di Samarra, Iraq. PhD bahasa Arab, Universiti Ain Shams, Kaherah (1968); profesor nahu di Universiti Baghdad kira-kira 40 tahun; ahli al-Majma' al-'Ilmi al-'Iraqi (Akademi Sains Iraq); pengarang Ma'ani an-Nahw dan at-Ta'bir al-Qur'ani; dikenali melalui rancangan TV Lamasat Bayaniyyah.

About the speaker: born 1933 in Samarra, Iraq. PhD in Arabic language, Ain Shams University, Cairo (1968); professor of grammar at the University of Baghdad for about 40 years; member of the Iraqi Academy of Sciences; author of Ma'ani al-Nahw and al-Ta'bir al-Qur'ani; widely known through the TV programme Lamasat Bayaniyya.
